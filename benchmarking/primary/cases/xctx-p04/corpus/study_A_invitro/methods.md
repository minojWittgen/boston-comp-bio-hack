# Study A — methods excerpt (synthetic)

Primary human epidermal keratinocytes (primary human epidermal keratinocytes, pooled 3 donors) were stimulated with IL-23 10 ng/mL, 24 h or vehicle (PBS/0.1% BSA), 24 h.
Total RNA was profiled by bulk RNA-seq (3 stimulated, 3 vehicle wells). Differential
expression was computed with DESeq2 for the contrast **IL-23 vs vehicle**; results are in `de_results.csv`
(log2FoldChange > 0 means higher in IL-23). Endpoint: mRNA abundance. Gene symbols are human (HGNC).
